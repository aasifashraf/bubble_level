Privacy Policy

Bubble Level & Angle Finder · by Aasif Ashraf · Effective 5 October 2026

In short: the app collects no personal data. Everything it measures stays on your phone. It has no ads, no analytics, no accounts, and it does not use the internet.

Who we are

Bubble Level & Angle Finder (“the app”) is developed and published by Aasif Ashraf, an independent developer. Contact: contact@aasifashraf.dev.

Data we collect

None. The app does not collect, store on any server, sell or share personal information. The Android release of the app does not even have permission to use the internet, so it cannot send anything anywhere.

Sensors

The tools read your phone’s motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope), magnetometer, barometer and light sensor to show levels, angles, the compass, metal detection, vibration, altitude and brightness. Sensor readings are processed on your phone only while a tool is open, and are never stored or sent.

Microphone

  • Only the Sound meter uses the microphone, and only while it is open on screen.
  • It asks for your permission first and explains why.
  • Sound is turned into a loudness level in the phone’s memory and immediately discarded. Audio is never recorded, saved or sent.
  • You can turn the permission off at any time in your phone’s settings; the other tools keep working.

What is stored on your phone

Your settings (theme, units, language, sounds and vibration) and the calibrations you make in the Level and the Ruler are saved on your phone so they are there next time. They never leave the phone, are not backed up by the app, and are deleted when you uninstall it.

Google Play

On Android the app may ask, through Google Play’s in-app review feature, whether you would like to rate it. That prompt is shown and handled by Google Play under Google’s Privacy Policy; the app itself receives no information about you or your review. Downloads and purchases through an app store are governed by that store’s privacy policy.

Children

The app is a general measuring tool, not directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date. If the app ever starts collecting data, this policy will say so before that happens.
